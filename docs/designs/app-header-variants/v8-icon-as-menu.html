Four window geometries

no ellipsis, no floating state, identity kept

The identity stays in the bar on every platform: it is what Windows and Linux have instead of a menu bar naming the app, and on macOS it is the mark beside the traffic lights. The rail keeps exactly the utilities it has today, update indicator included. The bar still reserves only the OS zone, so macOS is flush right and Windows flush left.

The rule follows the panel

drag it · 200 to 420px

The bar's leading cluster is the thread panel. Its last pixel is the panel's edge, and the rule that closes it runs on down the panel's border, so the header and the body share one line instead of two that nearly meet. Drag it: the wordmark gives way first, then the view's actions fold one at a time, and nothing ever spills out of the column. On the layouts whose controls sit on the left the column looks smaller than it is, because the lights take 80 to 138px of it, so at the app's 200px minimum the identity is the mark alone on macOS and Linux-left and still holds about five characters on Windows.

The truncation ladder

200 · 264 · 320 · 420

The same bar at four panel widths, on the two platforms whose identity differs: macOS reads "cio" and reaches its whole label sooner, Windows reads "CodeInOven" and holds a truncated wordmark across a wider range. Neither ever moves the rule off the panel's edge, and neither ever pushes a control into the title.

The icon carries both menus

left click project · right click thread

One control, two menus. The icon earns a hover plate so it reads as pressable, and a three-dot corner cue so a second menu is discoverable. Hover, focus and the closed state are the same target: 24px, which is the smallest a click target should be in a 48px bar.
