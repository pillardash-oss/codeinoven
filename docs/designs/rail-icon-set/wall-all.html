Ships and refined, together

238 drawn glyphs. Left is the shipping drawing; right is the refinement pass with square caps and corners, miter joins, and gold-ready solid anchors. Radius 6 organic forms and the browser mark keep their original shape. The hand redraws for note and tools, plus the corrected task-manager fit, carry over from wall.html. Hover lights a glyph; each tile anchors at #g-<id> for your notes.
