CodeInOven · brand work · round nine

CIO icons

The icon family has a name and a standard. Every rail, dock and header glyph in the app is rebuilt to that standard here, from the Lucide source it was using, and the page shows each one at the size it actually appears.

1 · The standard

one box, one stroke, from round three's five rules and round four's measurement
  • Box, 22.6 units of ink. Lucide draws inside 22.0. Round four tested targets from 22.0 to 23.1 and found 22.6 is the largest that keeps solid ink off the outermost row, so it is the standard rather than a preference.
  • Stroke, 1.9 units on every glyph. Under Lucide's 2, so the chrome reads as the lighter layer rather than a heavier one.
  • Corners, 1.6 to 2 units, matching the assistant mark's language.
  • Floor, 1.5 units. Nothing drawn thinner or closer than that, because at 16px one unit under 1.5 stops surviving the rasterizer.
  • Fit, every glyph fills the box. Each glyph is scaled about its own ink centre, so the rails stop looking uneven. This is pass F's construction applied to the whole inventory instead of the left rail alone.

The construction is pass F's: the source geometry sits in a group scaled to the box, and the group carries its own stroke-width of 1.9 divided by that scale, so the transform scales the drawing without scaling the stroke. That is what holds one stroke weight across glyphs of different sizes.

The chevrons, and the alternative

an indicator is a direction, not a picture

Back and forward are the only two glyphs whose source geometry is small enough that filling the box asks for a 1.72x scale. At that scale a chevron measures 22.6 units of ink while the search glyph beside it measures 22.6, so the direction arrows read as much larger controls than everything around them. A chevron keeps Lucide's own relative size instead, held at the family scale, which is 14.3 units of ink. Both are below, so the call is yours to overrule.

8 · Still open

  • The solid anchor. Round three's fifth rule gives every glyph one solid mass so it reads at 16px. The left rail's seven already carry one, drawn by hand in pass E. The other glyphs keep whatever solid accents Lucide gave them, and the rest are drawn as outlines, so an anchoring pass over the remaining glyphs is still owed.
  • The chevrons. Back and forward take Lucide's own relative size rather than filling the box, because at 1.72x they read as larger controls than the glyphs beside them. Both drawings are in section 3, so the exception is one number in inventory.ts if you want it gone.
  • The update button. One control cycling six states, still waiting on what that control is rather than how it is drawn.
