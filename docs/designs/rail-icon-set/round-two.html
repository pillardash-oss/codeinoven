CodeInOven · brand work · round two

The rail icon set, merged

Round one drew an outline pass and a solid pass. You picked the solid feel, and you are right about why: mass is what makes a mark recognisable at 16px. What the outline pass had was the drawing, the quieter and clearer shapes. So round two keeps the mass and rebuilds the drawing, and both new passes are single-colour, with no ember. The assistant mark is not touched or redrawn: it is the one custom icon the rail already has, and everything else now has to earn its place beside it.

2 · Direction C · The Weighted Line

the wire's drawing, at slab weight

The drawing language of round one's outline pass, redrawn so it cannot disappear. Two stroke weights instead of one: 3 units for the shape and 2.2 to 2.6 for the detail, which at 16px lands on 2 whole pixels and about 1.5. Round caps and joins, and rounded corners, because the assistant mark is rounded and the rail should not fight it. What it borrows from the slab is the solid anchor: one filled form per glyph, the thing your eye actually lands on. The lit door, the region you selected, the live entry, the two filter handles.

3 · Direction D · The Cut Block

the slab's mass, drawn with the wire's discipline

Still solid, but no longer a brick. Round one's slab cut rectangular holes into rectangular plates, which is why it read as a stamp rather than a drawing. Here the openings are rounded and generous, each one a shape you can name, and the silhouettes carry meaning of their own: Project is the folder with its tab, Thread is a stack of entries that taper as they age, Scoped is a region selected inside a field, Browser is a slab with one window. The material stays heavy and the drawing stops being blunt. This is the pass that keeps what you liked.

4 · In the button it will live in

the real 32px control, idle and current, in both themes

A rail item is a 32px button that sits on the surface when idle and on the elevated plate with a 2px bar when it is the current view. These are the real states, in the dark theme and then the light one, because the marks have to survive both without being recoloured.

5 · The trade

round two, stated plainly
C · The Weighted Line outline, heavy
  • Keeps round one's drawing clarity and fixes its only real failure, which was weight.
  • Sits closest to the assistant mark, so the rail reads as one family instead of a mascot with strangers beside it.
  • Borrows the slab's solid anchor, so it still has a shape for the eye to catch at 16px.
  • Risk: more parts per glyph than the slab, so the roomier ones (tasks, chat) are the busiest marks in the set.
Best if the rail should sit quietly and let the assistant mark stay the loudest thing in it.
D · The Cut Block solid, refined
  • Keeps the mass that made the slab recognisable, which was the whole point of your read.
  • Replaces the brick with a die-cut, so every opening is a named shape rather than a rectangle.
  • Its silhouettes carry meaning: the folder tab, the tapering entries, the selected region, the single window.
  • Risk: the heaviest pass of the four, so it will out-shout the Lucide icons in every other panel until those are replaced too.
Best if the rail should lead the identity rather than follow it.

What happens either way. The winner becomes one surface behind the rail's icon slot, eleven meanings, still reversible in a single commit. The assistant mark is untouched in both, and rounding is now available to the rest of the app, since it turned out we are not against it. Round one is still on the served page at index.html for comparison.
