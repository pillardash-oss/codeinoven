CodeInOven · brand work · round four

Right-sized, and three browsers

You were right about the size, and it was not only the globe. Every glyph in the set was drawn inside a smaller footprint than Lucide uses, which is why our globe looked smaller than Lucide's globe at the same 16px. Pass F fixes that with a measurement rather than an eye: each glyph is scaled about its own centre until its ink spans the same footprint Lucide spans, plus the extra you asked for. The drawing, the weight and every optical placement you already approved are untouched. Then three browser candidates, since that one is still a real question.

CodeInOven measured, not guessed

1 · The size finding

ink footprint, in the 24-unit design grid

Lucide draws inside a 20-unit square with a 2-unit stroke, so its icons carry 22 units of ink. Pass E was drawn inside 17 to 19 units, and the globe was 18.5. That is the whole of the size problem, and it explains why it felt like a family mismatch rather than a drawing fault: our glyphs were correct in shape and wrong in scale.

On the exact number you asked for. You said larger than Lucide by .05. I read that as five percent, which puts the target at 23.1 units, and 23.1 is not available: at that size the round glyphs (Scoped, Tasks, the globe) reach the edge of the 24-unit box and the stroke gets trimmed where it meets the boundary. I found this by testing targets from 22.0 to 23.1 and detecting solid ink on the outermost pixel row. 22.6 is the largest that does not clip, which is Lucide plus 2.7 percent rather than five. If you want the full five percent, the fix is to draw the set on a 26-unit grid instead of 24, and I will do that rather than ship a clipped glyph.

2 · Browser, three candidates

all at the corrected F scale

Yes, it can be custom, and it should be, because it is the one rail meaning that competes with the most recognisable icon in the world. All three are shown at 16px, the size the rail actually draws, then at 64px so the drawing can be judged. The constraint they all obey: no framed rectangle with a horizontal band, because you already taught me that is a bank card.

The globe what you saw
now 22.6 x 22.6, Lucide is 22.0 x 22.0

Carries no risk and no ownership. It is instantly understood and it is now correctly sized, so the size complaint is fully answered. The cost is that a wire globe is not a brand, and this glyph will look like every other app's globe in a screenshot.

The tab unique
22.5 x 21.5

Names what the view actually is. The rail's Browser is a set of tabs above a page, so the mark is a page with a tab rising out of its own top edge. No other icon in the set and no Lucide icon leaves its bounding box like this, because the shape is drawn as one continuous line rather than a stack of boxes. The lit line inside is the page it holds. This is the most honest of the three and the most clearly ours.

The compass rose unique
22.5 x 22.5

The world as a thing you navigate. A compass rose inscribed in a sphere, with the equator across it. Everyone reads it as the web immediately, and it is ours in a way a plain globe is not, because a circle with a meridian is every app's globe while a circle with a rose is a direction. The risk is that at 16px the four points sit close to the equator line and it is the busiest of the three.
